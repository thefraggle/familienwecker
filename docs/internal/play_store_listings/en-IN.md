## Full Description (4000 characters max)

<b>Your smart family schedule manager and alarm clock for a stress-free morning!</b> ⏰

Everyone needs to leave at the same time, the bathroom is occupied, and someone always ends up late. Sound familiar? It was my life too — every single morning. That's why I built <b>FamWake</b> as a father: the shared alarm clock I'd always wished we had. Whether you need a reliable family schedule manager or just want to optimize your daily wake up time, FamWake makes it easy with multiple alarms in one app.

FamWake calculates the <b>perfect wake up time</b> for each family member — based on bathroom needs, breakfast plans, and departure times. Everyone sleeps as long as possible, and nobody is late.

<b>🚿 Automatic Bathroom Schedule</b>
FamWake schedules who uses the bathroom and when — automatically, with zero overlaps. You only get woken up when it's actually your turn. No more waking up early "just in case."

<b>☕ Breakfast & Morning Routine</b>
Turn on the breakfast option and FamWake plans a shared window where everyone sits down together — before the rush begins.

<b>👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Your Whole Family at a Glance</b>
The dashboard shows the full morning plan in real-time: who wakes up when, who's in the bathroom, and when everyone leaves. Rearrange the order with a simple drag & drop.

<b>😴 Already Awake?</b>
One tap on "Already Awake" and your alarm won't ring — you're already up. Don't need an alarm today? Simply pause yourself and FamWake automatically recalculates the schedule for everyone else.

<b>📲 Push Notifications</b>
When someone changes their alarm or the order, you'll be notified instantly via push — so the whole family always stays up to date.

<b>🔔 Reliable Alarm</b>
FamWake wakes you even when the app is closed — on time, every time. Choose your own alarm sound and snooze when you need to.

<b>⏱️ Schedule Conflicts? Solved!</b>
When things get tight, FamWake detects it automatically and shows you exactly what needs adjusting — keeping everyone's morning smooth.

<b>🎨 Beautiful Design</b>
Switch between light and dark mode — or let it match your phone automatically. Sleek and easy on the eyes.

<b>🌍 25 Languages</b>
FamWake is available in English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Português, Русский, Українська, Türkçe, Svenska, Norsk, Dansk, 日本語, 한국어, 中文, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, বাংলা, मराठी, हिन्दी — and even Schwäbisch, Schweizerdeutsch, and Ruhrpott-Deutsch!

<b>🔒 Your Data Stays Private</b>
• No account needed: Use the app completely anonymously. Only the person creating the family needs to log in.
• No ads — ever.

<b>How It Works — 3 Simple Steps:</b>
1️⃣ Create a family and invite your loved ones with a join code.
2️⃣ Everyone enters their bathroom time, breakfast preference, and departure time.
3️⃣ FamWake calculates the perfect schedule — automatically, every morning.
